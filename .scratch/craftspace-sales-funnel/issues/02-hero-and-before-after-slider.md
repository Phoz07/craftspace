# 02: Hero Section and Interactive Before/After Comparison Slider

**What to build:** An impactful visual storytelling hero section featuring an interactive Before & After image comparison slider. Users can drag or swipe a divider across the image to seamlessly compare an empty bare shell concrete room against a completed Japandi built-in living space. A prominent Call to Action smoothly scrolls visitors straight into the estimation tool.
