# 04: Interactive 4-Step Cost Estimator Wizard UI

**What to build:** An intuitive 4-step wizard form guiding prospective clients through property classification, floor area sizing, decoration zone selection, and material grade tiers. Features an animated live counter that recalculates the price range instantaneously as options are toggled, with progress tracking and guard clauses ensuring high conversion and user trust.
